In this short instalment of the **Artistes** series, Anna Voitiuk speaks about her printmaking and presents **Haste makes waste**.

The work received **First Prize** in the **Jean de La Fontaine category** of the second *Hommage à Trois* graphic-art competition in 2021. Associació Internacional Duana de les Arts — AIDA published the video on 14 January 2022.

  <img src="/assets/img/publications/haste-makes-waste.jpg" alt="Anna Voitiuk’s print Haste makes waste, depicting the tortoise and the hare in an unusually shaped composition" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="885" height="732">
  <figcaption>Anna Voitiuk, <i>Haste makes waste</i>, 2021. Etching, linocut and embossing, 11 × 16 cm. Reproduction from the competition’s official catalogue.</figcaption>

At the time of the recording, Anna was living and working in Lviv and studying at the Ukrainian Academy of Printing. She explains that she chose the Jean de La Fontaine category because animals frequently appear in her prints, and describes her preference for unusually shaped plates that do not confine the composition to a rectangle.

**Haste makes waste** is her interpretation of **The Hare and the Tortoise**. The composition focuses on the moment when the hare realises that haste and underestimating his opponent have already cost him the race.
