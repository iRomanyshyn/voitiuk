  <h2>Website language</h2>
  <p>After you choose the English or Ukrainian version, the website stores a <code>voitiuk-language</code> preference in your browser’s local storage. It is used only to reopen your chosen language version and is not used for analytics or tracking. You can always reopen the language selector through the “Choose language” link in the footer.</p>

  <h2>Optional analytics</h2>
  <p>The website loads Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate website use and identify usability problems. Clarity can measure page views and interactions such as clicks and scrolling, and may create session recordings. It is not used for advertising on this website.</p>
  <p>Before you make a choice, Clarity receives a no-consent signal and operates in a limited mode without analytics or advertising cookies. If you choose “Allow analytics”, analytics cookies help Clarity connect page views into more complete sessions; advertising storage remains disabled. The website continues to work normally if you decline.</p>
  <p>Your analytics choice is stored in your browser’s local storage so the website can remember it; this marker is not used for tracking. Microsoft processes data received by Clarity under its own privacy terms.</p>
  <p><a href="https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/clarity/setup-and-installation/clarity-cookies" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Learn more about Clarity cookies</a> and read the <a href="https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Microsoft Privacy Statement</a>.</p>
  <p><button class="button button-secondary" type="button" data-clarity-settings>Review analytics choice</button></p>

  <h2>Contact form</h2>
  <p>When you submit the contact form, the name, email address and message you provide are sent to Formspree so Anna Voitiuk can reply. Formspree receives, stores and processes the submission to deliver it.</p>
  <p>By submitting the form, you use Formspree to deliver your message. Formspree’s handling of the submitted data is governed by its <a href="https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Privacy Policy</a>, and use of the service by its <a href="https://formspree.io/legal/terms-of-service/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Terms of Service</a>. Do not send sensitive personal information through the form.</p>

  <h2>External links</h2>
  <p>The website links to Etsy, Instagram, Facebook, galleries and professional sources. Those services have their own privacy policies.</p>
  <p>Pages with video initially show a locally hosted preview. The embedded YouTube player loads only after you press play; from that point, your interaction with it is governed by the <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Privacy Policy</a> and the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/static?template=terms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">YouTube Terms of Service</a>.</p>
