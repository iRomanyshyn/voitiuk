  <h2>Optional analytics</h2>
  <p>This website loads Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate use and identify usability problems. Clarity can measure page views and interactions such as clicks and scrolling and can provide session recordings. It is not used here for advertising.</p>
  <p>Before you choose, Clarity receives a denied consent signal and operates in a limited mode without analytics or advertising cookies. If you choose “Allow analytics”, analytics cookies are enabled so Clarity can connect page views into fuller sessions; advertising storage remains denied. If you decline, the website continues to work normally.</p>
  <p>Your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage so the website can remember it; that preference is not used to track you. Microsoft processes Clarity data under its own privacy terms.</p>
